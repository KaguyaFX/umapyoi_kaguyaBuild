## Gameplay at a glance

1. Place Carats or Umamusume Tickets on an Umamusume Pedestal to get a *Faded Umamusume Soul*.
   Put a book on the pedestal instead to turn it into a support card pedestal.
2. Bring the faded soul to the **Three Goddesses Statue**. Add two *Umamusume Spirits* from
   different Umamusume to inherit their factors at the same time.
3. Hold the *Umamusume Soul* to become that Umamusume, with the soul's stats, skills and mood.
4. Put the soul and up to six support cards into the **Training Terminal**.
5. Enter races at the **Race Registration Terminal** with a race ticket for rewards.
6. Retire the soul at the **Retire Register Lectern** to receive its *Spirit*,
   then pass its factors on to the next soul at the Three Goddesses Statue.

The in-game *Tracen Trainers' Manual* (needs Patchouli) explains each step in more detail.
